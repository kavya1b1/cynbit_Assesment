# AI Opportunity Report — Cynbit GrowthDesk Pro

## Executive summary
Cynbit publicly describes services spanning AI automation, custom software, cloud infrastructure and digital marketing/CRM automation. GrowthDesk proposes a lead qualification and discovery workspace that turns inbound enquiries into structured briefs for human review. This is an independent assessment prototype, not an existing Cynbit product, and it uses no private customer data.

## Opportunity hypothesis
Inbound enquiries can vary in intent, completeness and urgency. A sales or presales team may need to interpret each message, map it to a service, extract requirements, identify missing details, prepare discovery questions and draft a response. Standardizing this preparation may reduce handling time and improve qualification consistency. This hypothesis must be validated with Cynbit staff; no claim is made that Cynbit currently has this problem.

## Proposed workflow
1. Accept an enquiry.
2. Classify it into a service line.
3. Extract explicitly stated requirements using deterministic NLP or optional LLM assistance.
4. Calculate a transparent priority score.
5. Detect missing details.
6. Suggest discovery questions and next action.
7. Draft a follow-up email for human review.
8. Display pipeline, service demand and priority insights.

## Expected value and KPIs
No realized ROI is claimed. A pilot should measure median triage time, qualification completeness, staff acceptance/override rate, human-rated discovery brief quality, and qualified-lead-to-meeting conversion against a baseline. Illustrative pilot targets such as 30% lower preparation time or 85% reviewer acceptance are hypotheses, not measured outcomes.

## Risks and controls
Incorrect classification: show matched terms and permit override. LLM hallucination: extract only stated facts and require review. Misleading score: show weights and never call it conversion probability. Data exposure: minimize data and apply access/retention controls. Unapproved outreach: draft-only; no sending integration exists. Workflow mismatch: validate with sales/presales before a pilot.
